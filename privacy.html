Privacy policy

Last updated 23 September 2026. This policy covers the Symbiosis application, an internal tool of PREREAL LTD, 1 Bull Cottage, Main Street, Upton, Huntingdon PE28 5YB, England (company number 14624245).

Who uses it

Only PREREAL LTD's own director and bookkeeper. There are no external users and the application is not distributed.

What data it accesses

Through the Intuit QuickBooks Online API, and only for PREREAL LTD's own company file: accounts, bank register transactions, invoices, customers and suppliers, and financial reports. It does not access QuickBooks Payments or Payroll.

Where data is kept

QuickBooks data is read on demand and is not copied into any external service. Working notes derived from it are kept in a private repository controlled by the company. OAuth tokens are stored on one company computer, encrypted at rest by the operating system, and are not shared.

Third parties

Data is not sold, shared or transferred to any third party. The company's appointed accountant receives reports in the normal course of preparing the statutory accounts.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time by the company from its QuickBooks Online account (Apps, Connected apps) or by disconnecting through the application. Revoking access deletes the stored tokens.

Your rights and contact

PREREAL LTD is the data controller. Questions or requests: accounts@prereal.co.uk. UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 apply; complaints may be made to the Information Commissioner's Office.
