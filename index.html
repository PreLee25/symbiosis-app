Symbiosis for PREREAL LTD

Symbiosis is a private, internal bookkeeping assistant used by PREREAL LTD (company number 14624245, England and Wales) to read and maintain its own QuickBooks Online company file. It is operated by the company's director on a single computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does: reads the chart of accounts, bank register, invoices, customers and financial reports; creates, updates and deletes the company's own transactions under the director's instruction; produces month-end reports for the company's accountant.

What it does not do: it does not take payments, run payroll, access any other company's data, or share data with third parties.
